Eight weeks.

Weeks 1–3

Build

Assemble a humanoid from 3D-printed parts and servo motors, one limb at a time.

Week 4

Wire

Power, an onboard computer, sensors. Every connection checked before the first power-on.

Weeks 5–6

Code

Calibrate every joint, then write the first program: move joint 3 by 10 degrees, and explain why it doesn’t land exactly there.

Week 7

Simulate

Rebuild the robot in the MuJoCo physics simulator. Run the same motion in both worlds and compare.

Week 8

Learn

A first taste of reinforcement learning: change one reward, and watch the robot behave differently.

Showcase

Show

Demo the robot as a team, and explain what worked, what didn’t, and why.
